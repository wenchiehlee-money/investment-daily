### Global market

- Hyperscaler / CSP capex and AI infrastructure spending
- Major geopolitical or policy developments with direct market impact

## Daily analytical lens

For important items, answer:

1. **What happened?** — verified facts and numbers.
2. **Why does it matter?** — mechanism linking the event to earnings, valuation, liquidity, or sentiment.
3. **Who benefits / loses?** — relevant companies, sectors, or themes.
4. **Time horizon** — short-term price effect vs. medium/long-term thesis.
5. **What to watch next?** — catalysts, confirmations, invalidation points, and risks.

## Writing conventions

- Primary language: Traditional Chinese
- Timezone: Asia/Taipei
- Use exact dates for time-sensitive events
- Separate confirmed facts from inference
- Prefer primary sources, filings, earnings releases/calls, exchange disclosures, and high-quality financial journalism
- Include source links for material factual claims
- Clearly label uncertainty when evidence is incomplete
